I chose Mystery Egg Hunt and expanded it into a mini adventure. Players tap eggs to discover clues, then unlock a sealed golden egg with a final animation + message. It starts simple and gets more magical as they interact.

Interaction: each egg click reveals a clue and fills progress. Once all clues are found, the final egg cracks open, confetti appears, and a celebration message is revealed.

Bonus hidden trigger: scroll down to wake up the bunny guide.
